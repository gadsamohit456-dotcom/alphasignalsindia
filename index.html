FAQ

What time is the signal sent every day?
Signal is sent at 9:45 AM IST every trading day (Mon-Fri excluding expiry days). Morning briefing at 8:30 AM.
Why do you skip expiry days?
Expiry days (Wednesday) are highly unpredictable. Our system skips them to protect capital. Discipline over gambling.
What is the Confidence Score?
Every signal gets a 0-100% confidence score based on RSI, MACD, candle patterns, global markets and VIX. Only enter when confidence is above 65%.
Is this SEBI registered advice?
No. AlphaSignals India is an educational platform only. All signals are for learning purposes. Always trade at your own risk.
How much capital do I need?
Our risk calculator recommends max 2% capital risk per trade. You can start with as little as ₹5,000-10,000 for 1 lot options.
